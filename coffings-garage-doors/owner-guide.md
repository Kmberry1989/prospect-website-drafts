# Your Website — Owner Guide
**Coffing's Ro-Way Door Sales & Service Inc** · Logansport, Indiana

This is your new website. Here's what you can change yourself, anytime — no tech skills needed.

## What you can edit

- **Announcement banner** — the strip at the very top of the page. Use it for holiday hours, weather closures, or timely notices. Leave it blank and it disappears from the site automatically.
- **Specials** — add, change, or remove offers (like the current "Free estimates" offer).
- **Services** — add, remove, or reword anything on your services list.
- **Hours** — update open/close times for any day of the week.
- **Business info** — your business name, tagline, phone number, and address. The call buttons and the directions link update automatically when you change these.

## How to make changes

Kyle will set up a private admin page at **/admin/** on your live website. He'll give you a password and an access token — keep them somewhere safe. Log in there, make your edits, hit save, and the live site updates right away.
