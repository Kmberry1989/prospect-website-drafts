# Owner Guide — Mr. B's Laundry and Tanning

This is your website's instruction sheet. Keep it somewhere handy.

## What you can edit yourself

Once your site is live, you can change all of this without calling anyone:

- **Announcement banner** — a short notice at the very top of the page (holiday hours, weather closures, special events). Leave it blank and the banner hides itself.
- **Specials** — real offers and promotions (title + short description). Add or remove them anytime.
- **Services** — your service list: names, descriptions, and prices if you publish them.
- **Hours** — your hours of operation, shown in a table.
- **Business info** — your name, tagline, phone number, and address. The Call button and Get Directions link update automatically when you change these.

## How to edit

Your site has a shared **admin page** at **/admin/** on your live site address.

1. Go to the /admin/ page on your site.
2. Sign in with the password Kyle set up for you.
3. Make your changes — they save automatically.

Kyle Berry sets up the admin page, password, and access token for you before handoff. He can also walk you through it once by phone.

## Things to double-check before going live

- Hours follow the shop's own Google description: last wash and tan at 7:00 PM, doors to 8:00 PM. A couple of stale listings claim 7 AM–10 PM — ignore those unless the shop confirms a change.
