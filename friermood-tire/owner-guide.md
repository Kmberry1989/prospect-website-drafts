# Owner Guide — Friermood Tire

This one-page guide covers what you can change on your website yourself.

## What you can edit

Everything on your site's pages is editable — no coding needed:

- **Business info** — name, tagline, phone number, address
- **Announcement** — the banner at the top of the page (holiday hours, weather closures, hiring notices — leave it blank to hide it)
- **Specials** — add, change, or remove offers anytime
- **Services** — add, rename, or remove services and their descriptions
- **Hours** — update opening times whenever they change

## How to edit it

1. Go to the **/admin/** page on your live site (Kyle will give you the exact link).
2. Sign in with the password Kyle sets up for you.
3. Make your changes and save — they go live on your site right away.

Kyle sets up the admin page for you with a password and a secure access token, so only you can make changes.

## What you can't break

Editing only changes the words on the page. The design, colors, and layout stay exactly as they are — you can't accidentally break the look of the site.
