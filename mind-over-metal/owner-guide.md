# Owner's Guide — Mind Over Metal LLC Website

**Kyle Berry** · kylematthewberry@gmail.com — call or email if anything below doesn't make sense.

## What you can change yourself

- **Business info** — your name, tagline, phone number, and address
- **Announcement banner** — a one-line message across the top (e.g. what kinds of jobs you're taking)
- **Specials** — add, edit, or remove specials anytime
- **Services** — add or remove services (welding types, fabrication, repair)
- **Hours** — add your hours here whenever you want them shown; right now the site says to call the shop because no published hours were found

## How to make changes

Kyle sets up a private **admin page** at **/admin/** on the live site. He gives you a **password** and an **access token** — keep both private. Open the admin page on your phone or computer, sign in, make your edits, and save. The live site updates automatically.

## What you don't need to worry about

- The design, colors, and layout — that's Kyle's part
- Keeping the site online — the hosting handles that

## When to contact Kyle

- You lost the admin password or the access token
- Something looks broken on the site
- You want a new section added (photos, a contact form, a map, etc.)
