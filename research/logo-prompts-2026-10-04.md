Flat vector-style logo mark, simple bold silhouette readable at 32px, 1-2 solid colors, no fine text, no gradients, no photorealism. An original mark for the business — never copying an existing brand.

Header standard: `.biz-logo { max-height: 104px; width: auto; max-width: 72%; }`, 80px on mobile, always with alt text. Low-res sources (<300px) are never displayed above native size.
