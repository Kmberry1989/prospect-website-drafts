# Proposal Kits v2 — "Remarkably Affordable" pricing (2026-10-04)

Companion to `proposal-kits-2026-10-04.md` (v1), which it does NOT replace. v1 keeps the original pricing framework, full competitor research, domains, and differentiators — everything below references it. v2 re-prices every package under the new structure.

## The new pricing (applies to all kits below)

- **Starter — $499** (includes 3 months care): up to 5 pages, Google Business Profile setup/optimization, tap-to-call, directions, hours block.
- **Business — $999** (includes 6 months care): up to 15 pages, GBP, QR table-menu system OR online booking setup, social starter pack (profiles + 10 launch posts), review-request cards with QR.
- **Complete — $1,250** (includes 12 months care): unlimited pages, everything in Business, plus logo design/refresh, menu redesign, 250 business cards designed (print at cost), QR table tents.
- **After the included period:** $49/month or $490/year (two months free) — hosting, small content changes, GBP monitoring, "just text me" updates.
- **À la carte:** logo $199 · menu redesign $249 · business card design $99 · photo refresh visit $99.

Deliberately ~half of local agency rates. Page count is never the objection — pages are cheap on a static site.
