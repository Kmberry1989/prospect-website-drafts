## Rules baked into the process

1. Real logos always win — never generate a replacement for a business that has one.
2. Low-res sources (<300px) are never displayed above their native size; the owner is asked for a high-res file at onboarding (it's in the proposal kit checklist now).
3. Profile photos are not logos — flagged, not silently accepted.
4. New header logos: max-height 104px desktop / 80px mobile, width auto, always alt text.
5. Generated logo prompts (separate file) produce flat vector-style marks: simple bold silhouette, readable at 32px, 1–2 colors, no fine text, no gradients — and they are always presented to the owner as a starting point, never as the business's real identity.
