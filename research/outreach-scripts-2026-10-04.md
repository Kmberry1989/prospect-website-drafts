# Outreach scripts (2026-10-04)

## The universal frame (every call and visit)

You're not selling a website. You're showing them something that already exists — their business, looking good, on your phone. The draft does the selling. Your job: get 30 seconds, show the draft, ask for 10 more minutes.

Three beats, every time:
1. **Who I am** — "Kyle Berry, I'm a web designer here in Kokomo."
2. **What I built for THEM** — "I built a draft website for [Business] — your real menu, your hours, your photos. It's on my phone right now."
3. **The ask** — "Can I show it to you? Thirty seconds."

## The in-person flow

1. **Open the QR sheet** on your phone (`/qr/` page). Hand them your phone or have them scan the code for THEIR business with their camera. Their draft opens on THEIR phone — that's the moment. Let them scroll. Don't talk over it.
2. **Let them react.** They'll say something about a photo, the menu, the colors. Agree, then: "That's all your real stuff — your menu, your hours, your photos."
3. **Name the gap, once.** One sentence: "Right now when someone searches for [what they do] in [town], this is what they'd find instead — [nothing / a dead page / a directory]." Show them if you can.
4. **Walk the proposal kit.** Tier, every item relisted, price, included care months. "Most businesses land on [Business/Complete]."
5. **The close.** "I can have the finished version live [this week / next week]. Nothing due until you approve it. Want me to lock you in?"

## Objection handling

- **"How much?"** — "Three options: $499, $999, or $1,250 — most businesses end up at $999. Every one includes months of care at no extra charge, and you pay nothing until the finished site is live and you love it."
- **"I already have Facebook."** — "Facebook's great — this doesn't replace it. But you don't own your Facebook page, and half your customers never log in. This is yours, it shows up on Google, and it works for people who'll never open the app."
- **"I need to think about it."** — "Totally fair. I'll text you the link to your draft so you can look it over — [send it]. I'll check back Thursday. Sound good?" (Then actually check back Thursday. Log it in the tracker notes.)
- **"My nephew does computers."** — Smile. "Then he'll tell you this is a good deal. The draft's free either way — want to see it?"
- **"Business is fine without it."** — "That's exactly why this is cheap insurance. The customers you're NOT getting are the ones who searched, didn't find you, and called someone else — you never even knew they existed."
