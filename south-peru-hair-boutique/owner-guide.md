# Owner Guide — South Peru Hair Boutique & Skin Care Center

This page explains what you can change on your website yourself, and how.

## What you can edit yourself

- **Business info** — business name, tagline, phone number, address
- **Announcement** — the banner message at the top of the page (leave blank to hide it)
- **Specials** — add, change, or remove specials anytime
- **Services** — add, change, or remove services, descriptions, and prices
- **Hours** — update your hours whenever they change

## How to edit

Your site comes with a shared admin page at **/admin/** on the live site.
Kyle sets it up for you with a password and an access token, then shows you
where to sign in. Once you're in, every section above is a simple form —
make your changes and save, and the site updates right away. No coding needed.
